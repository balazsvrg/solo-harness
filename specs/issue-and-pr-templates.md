# Issue and PR templates

## Templates (R19)

- Issue form "Change request" (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/change-request.yml`): problem, acceptance ideas, proposed size.
- Issue form "Bug" (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug.yml`): expected vs actual, reproduction.
- PR template (`.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`): the `Size:`, `Change:` and `ADR:` fields and the optional `Docs:` field that `docs-gate` reads (see `verification.md`), a summary, "How I tested it" (manual-test notes) and the linked issue.
